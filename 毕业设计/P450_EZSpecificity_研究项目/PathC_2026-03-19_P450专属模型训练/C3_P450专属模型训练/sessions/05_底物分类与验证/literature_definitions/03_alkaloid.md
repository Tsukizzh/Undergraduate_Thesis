instead, they are connected with amino acid pathways where they are derived from by amination or transamination reaction from forerunners or postcursors of amino acid.\"\n\n**典型例子**: 咖啡因 (caffeine)、可可碱 (theobromine)、辣椒素 (capsaicin)、萜类生物碱 (terpenoid alkaloids 如 aconitine)、甾体生物碱 (steroid alkaloids 如 solanidine)\n\n**结论: 是的，它们是生物碱**。虽然氮不源自氨基酸，但通过酰胺化/转氨化反应引入氮，且具有生物碱的典型结构和生物活性特征。\n\n---\n\n## 5. 关键边界: Alkaloid vs Amino Acid Derivative\n\n### 总体原则\n\n**来源**: Wikipedia \"Alkaloid\" (综合多文献):\n\n> \"The boundary between alkaloids and other nitrogen-containing natural compounds is not clear-cut.\"\n\n### 具体化合物分析\n\n| 化合物 | 结构特征 | 分类 | 依据 |\n|--------|---------|------|------|\n| **色氨酸 (Tryptophan)** | 完整 NH2-CH(R)-COOH 骨架 | **氨基酸** (非生物碱) | IUPAC 明确排除氨基酸 |\n| **N-甲基色氨酸** | 仍有 COOH + alpha-氨基 | **氨基酸衍生物** (非生物碱) | 仍保留氨基酸骨架 |\n| **5-羟基色氨酸 (5-HTP)** | 仍有 COOH + alpha-氨基 | **氨基酸** (有争议) | PMC7796270 称其为 \"amino acid\"；PsychonautWiki 称其为 \"synthetic indole alkaloid molecule\"。分类取决于语境 |\n| **色胺 (Tryptamine)** | 脱羧产物，无 COOH | **原生物碱 / 简单吲哚生物碱** | Wikipedia Indole alkaloid: \"One of the simplest and yet widespread indole derivatives are the biogenic amines tryptamine and 5-hydroxytryptamine (serotonin).\" 但也注明: \"their assignment to the alkaloid is not universally accepted\" |\n| **血清素 (Serotonin, 5-HT)** | 5-羟基色胺 | **生物胺 / 边界化合物** | Wikipedia: \"Natural compounds containing nitrogen in the exocyclic position (mescaline, serotonin, dopamine, etc.) are usually classified as amines rather than as alkaloids.\" 但同时: \"Some amines, such as adrenaline and serotonin... are sometimes called alkaloids.\" |\n| **褪黑素 (Melatonin)** | N-乙酰-5-甲氧基色胺 | **生物胺 / 边界化合物** | 与血清素类似，通常归为生物胺 |\n| **多巴胺 (Dopamine)** | 酪氨酸脱羧产物 | **生物胺** | 通常归为 catecholamine，非生物碱 |\n\n### 关键转折点: 脱羧反应\n\n**来源**: JACS Au 2024 (PMC11350588), bioRxiv 2024:\n\n> Decarboxylation is \"the initial biosynthetic step\" in alkaloid biosynthesis. **核苷酸** (Nucleotides)\n5. **核酸** (Nucleic acids)\n6. **氨基糖** (Amino sugars)\n7. **抗生素** (Antibiotics)\n\n**来源**: Wikipedia \"Alkaloid\" -- 额外排除:\n\n> \"Compounds like amino acid peptides, proteins, nucleotides, nucleic acid, amines, and antibiotics are usually not called alkaloids.\"\n\n注意 Wikipedia 额外加入了 \"amines\"（简单胺类），IUPAC 原文中没有明确提及。\n\n**简单胺类** (如甲胺 methylamine、三甲胺 trimethylamine): 不满足 Pelletier \"cyclic\" 要求，且广泛分布，不被视为生物碱。\n\n**合成含氮药物**: 见下节。\n\n---\n\n## 7. \"天然来源\" 要求\n\n**来源**: IUPAC Gold Book:\n\n> \"occurring mostly in the plant kingdom\" -- 用的是 \"mostly\"，不是 \"exclusively\"\n\n**来源**: ScienceDirect Topics (Biomedicine & Pharmacotherapy, 2022):\n\n> \"Some synthetic compounds of similar structure may also be named alkaloids.\"\n\n**来源**: PMC8875739 (Molecules, 2022):\n\n> \"Few synthetic compounds are also considered as alkaloids too.\"\n\n**结论**: 天然来源不是绝对要求。具有生物碱典型结构的合成化合物也可以被称为生物碱，但这是 \"by extension\"（延伸用法），不是核心定义。\n\n---\n\n## 总结:
