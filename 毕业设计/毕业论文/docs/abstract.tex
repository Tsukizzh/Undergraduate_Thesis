\begin{abstract}
  酶底物特异性预测是生物催化、合成生物学与药物研发中的核心任务。2025 年发表于 \textit{Nature} 的 EZSpecificity 模型代表了该任务的当前最先进水平，论文声称其具有跨酶家族的通用预测能力。然而，EZSpecificity 的训练数据集 ESIBank 在细胞色素 P450 超家族上覆盖显著稀缺（$389$ 条，仅占 $1.5\%$），而 P450 在临床药物代谢、植物次生代谢与农药降解中具有极高应用价值，恰好是检验其通用性声明的理想试金石。针对这一问题，本研究开展了三方面工作。第一，从 RCSB PDB、ESIBank、P450Rdb、PlantP450DB 与 PCPD 五个互补来源整合构建 P450 专属基准数据集，经跨源去重合并后包含 $1{,}622$ 个唯一酶、$2{,}125$ 个唯一底物、$4{,}751$ 条正样本对与 $47{,}510$ 条可用训练评估样本；该数据集显著扩展了植物 P450 的数据覆盖。第二，以 InterPro 功能域严格识别 ESIBank 原始训练集中的 $389$ 个 P450 并从本研究测试集中过滤对应酶样本，建立泄漏控制的外部公平对比评估协议。第三，在原架构基础上引入残基级几何向量感知（GVP）通路构成双图架构，并以零初始化加延迟解冻的融合设计保证训练起步的基线等价性。核心实证结果为：在 $7{,}963$ 条过滤后外部测试集上，EZSpecificity 公开预训练模型仅取得 Test AUC $= 0.5586$、AUPR $= 0.1004$，后者接近正负比 $1{:}10.2$ 下的随机基线；本研究领域专属基线模型在相同测试集上达到 Test AUC $= 0.9205$、AUPR $= 0.6403$，相对参考模型的差距为 $\Delta\mathrm{AUC} = +0.3619$。双图架构在单随机切分评估下相对原架构观察到 Test AUC $+0.0008$ 的微幅提升、Test AUPR $-0.0275$ 的回落。研究表明通用 SOTA 模型在训练集稀缺家族上存在明显的 zero-shot 泛化瓶颈，领域专属数据的重训对 P450 特异性预测是必要的；双图架构作为方向信息感知设计的初步可行性探索为后续几何感知模型研究提供参考技术路线。
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
  Enzyme--substrate specificity prediction is a core task in biocatalysis, synthetic biology, and drug discovery. EZSpecificity, a cross-attention graph neural network published in \textit{Nature} in 2025, represents the current state of the art and claims generality across diverse enzyme families. However, its training dataset ESIBank substantially under-represents the cytochrome P450 superfamily: only 389 P450 enzymes ($1.5\%$ of ESIBank's 25{,}225 total enzymes) are included, whereas P450s are of exceptional importance in clinical drug metabolism, plant secondary metabolism, and pesticide degradation---making P450 an ideal stress test for the model's generality claim. To address this gap, this thesis pursues three lines of work. First, a P450-specific benchmark dataset was constructed by integrating five complementary sources (RCSB PDB, ESIBank, P450Rdb, PlantP450DB, and PCPD), yielding $1{,}622$ unique enzymes, $2{,}125$ unique substrates, $4{,}751$ deduplicated positive pairs, and $47{,}510$ trainable samples, with substantially expanded coverage of plant P450s. Second, a leakage-controlled external evaluation protocol was established by strictly identifying the 389 ESIBank-overlapping P450s through InterPro domain verification and filtering matching enzymes from the held-out test set. Third, a dual-graph architecture was designed on top of the original framework by introducing a residue-level Geometric Vector Perceptron (GVP) pathway, with a zero-initialization and delayed-unfreeze fusion design that preserves strict baseline equivalence at training step zero. Empirically, on the 7{,}963-sample filtered test set, the public EZSpecificity checkpoint achieved only Test AUC $= 0.5586$ and AUPR $= 0.1004$ (the latter close to the $\approx 0.09$ random baseline at $1{:}10.2$ class ratio), whereas our P450-specific baseline trained with identical architecture reached Test AUC $= 0.9205$ and AUPR $= 0.6403$, a gap of $\Delta\mathrm{AUC} = +0.3619$. The dual-graph model observed a marginal Test AUC improvement of $+0.0008$ and a Test AUPR drop of $-0.0275$ under single-fold evaluation, reported as a preliminary feasibility exploration of direction-aware architectural design. The thesis demonstrates that general-purpose state-of-the-art models exhibit a zero-shot generalization bottleneck on training-underrepresented enzyme families, and that domain-specific retraining is necessary for P450 substrate specificity prediction.
\end{abstract*}
